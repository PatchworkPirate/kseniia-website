About

Most projects don't fail because people aren't good at their jobs. They fail because everyone is busy doing their own job while nobody owns the space in between.

That's usually where I come in.

For the last nine years I've worked across agencies, corporate teams and live productions helping projects move from "someone should really coordinate this" to "it's done." I've managed packaging launches, broadcast operations and editorial productions. The work looks different every time. What stays the same is the approach: get clear on what needs to happen, build the structure and see it through.

I'm based in Cologne and work in German and English. I'm not a consultant who hands over a report — I'm the person who stays until the job is done.
